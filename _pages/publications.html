Current research

Where tasks compete inside deep networks

Since Sep 2026

Gradient-balancing methods such as FairGrad weigh tasks by comparing whole-network gradients. Benchmarking 26 multi-task gradient methods in one PyTorch trainer on NYUv2, CelebA and QM9, I found that a few small early layers carry most of the gradient energy, while in a QM9 message-passing network the energy targets separate only at the readout layer. I am developing a block kernel that balances each module’s contribution to the task weights.

Scaling TOPPO to VLA and LLM RL

Jun – Sep 2026

I set up RL fine-tuning for a 3.3B π0 vision-language-action model with RLinf, and built a GRPO/PPO pipeline for LLMs on verl, vLLM and FSDP2 with a 120B LLM judge as the reward model. Unlike on small networks, standard gradient surgery on the whole network showed no clear benefit on π0 or on LLMs. That is what sent me to look inside the network.

Runtime-aware HPC job scheduling

Since Oct 2026, with Rui Miao

I wrote slurmsim, a Python and C simulator that reproduces real Slurm’s job start order. Setting each job’s minimum time from a Kaplan–Meier estimate of its runtime, with the user’s limit kept as the hard cap, cut mean turnaround by 8–18% in simulation and by 8% in a replay of a national-lab trace, with almost no extra timeouts.
