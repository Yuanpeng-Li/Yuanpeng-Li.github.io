I am a Ph.D. student in Statistics at UC Irvine, advised by Prof. [Annie Qu](https://qu.pstat.ucsb.edu/) and Prof. [Rui Miao](https://rui-miao.github.io/). I received my B.S. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from Jilin University in 2024.

I work on reinforcement learning and multi-task optimization: training one model on many tasks without the hardest ones falling behind, from small control policies to VLA models and LLMs. My NeurIPS 2026 paper, [TOPPO](/toppo/), shows that in multi-task PPO the critic's gradients are badly imbalanced across tasks, and balances them. I now study how tasks compete inside deep networks. More on my [research](/research/).

## Outside research

**Robots.** I am learning robot learning hands-on: training locomotion policies for a Unitree Go2 quadruped in Isaac Lab and checking them sim-to-sim in MuJoCo, the last step before the real robot, and experimenting with humanoid motion tracking in simulation. I expect robotics to become part of my research.

**Linux, networks and agent harnesses.** I run my own small compute setup of headless Linux machines on a private network, and I enjoy building the harness around coding agents: skills, MCP servers, and the permission checks that let an agent use a shared cluster safely. Most of my [projects](/projects/) started there.
